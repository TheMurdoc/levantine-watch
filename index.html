Data provenance

Static snapshot. Everything on this page was aggregated from the public sources listed above at one point in time; nothing is live and nothing updates. GNSS hexes show reported accuracy degradation, not a confirmed cause. Places are commercial listings, not verified infrastructure. Vessel positions are AIS effort records, not real-time tracks. Wireless access points are shown as counts and positions only; network names are withheld. Cross-check every item before treating it as ground truth.
